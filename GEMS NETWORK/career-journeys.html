THE GEMS ROLE

More than a single answer. A trusted guide for the journey.

GEMS helps you understand what you need, determine your next step, and connect with trusted resources — at every stage of your career journey.

Guidance

Help visitors understand unfamiliar choices, processes, and next steps.

Planning

Help organize goals, priorities, timing, and practical considerations.

Preparation

Help visitors become better prepared for professional opportunities and transitions.

Connections

Connect visitors with trusted network providers when specialized expertise is needed.

Ongoing Support

Remain a resource as circumstances and goals evolve.

GEMS may provide certain consulting and guidance services directly. Specialized services may be delivered by qualified providers within the Trusted Network.

TRUSTED NETWORK RELATIONSHIP

Career Services And Trusted Network Relationship

  • GEMS helps the visitor understand what they need.
  • GEMS helps determine the appropriate next step.
  • Where appropriate, GEMS provides guidance or consulting directly.
  • Where specialist expertise is required, GEMS connects the visitor with an appropriate Trusted Network provider.
  • The value of the network is the confidence created by curated, trusted connections — not simply the number of providers.

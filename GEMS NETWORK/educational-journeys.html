The GEMS Role-What we Do

You don't have to know everything before you begin.

One of the hardest parts of a new journey is knowing what you don't know. GEMS helps you identify the questions, understand the options, plan the next step, and connect with trusted people when specialized help is needed.

Guidance

Help make unfamiliar decisions easier to understand.

PLANNING

Help organize the next steps, timing, priorities, and practical considerations.

CONNECTIONS

Connect students with trusted network providers when specialized services are required.

ONGOING SUPPORT

Remain available as the student's needs change from one stage of the journey to the next.

Important Note: GEMS may provide certain consulting and guidance services directly while connecting users to qualified third-party providers for specialized services. The page should never imply that GEMS itself performs every service listed.

Choose Your Membership

How closely would you like GEMS to travel with you?

Some students need a question answered. Others want someone they can return to throughout the journey. GEMS membership is designed to provide that continuing relationship.

Explorer

A starting point for discovering GEMS and available resources.

Perfect for students who are just beginning their journey and it Includes:

  • Educational articles and guides
  • Country and university information
  • Career pathway resources
  • Public webinars and events
  • Community news and updates
  • Access to selected free resources

Whether you're considering studying abroad, exploring career options, or simply gathering information, Explorer gives you access to the knowledge and tools to help you make informed decisions—at your own pace.
💎 Most Popular

Companion

For students who want more personalized guidance and support.

Ideal for students actively preparing to study abroad or build their future. Includes everything in Explorer, plus:

  • Personalized education roadmap
  • University and program comparisons
  • Visa preparation guidance
  • Application planning
  • Document checklists
  • Financial planning resources
  • Priority member support
  • Exclusive member resources

When you're ready to take the next step,Companion provides personalized guidance,planning tools,and ongoing support to help you stay organized and move forward with clarity.

Navigator

For students who want a deeper level of ongoing assistance.

Your journey becomes our journey. Includes everything in Companion, plus:

  • One-on-one consultations
  • Dedicated member assistance
  • Priority appointments
  • Application reviews
  • Family guidance sessions
  • Scholarship strategy
  • Personalized planning support
  • Concierge referrals
  • Ongoing journey support
  • Partner discounts and benefits

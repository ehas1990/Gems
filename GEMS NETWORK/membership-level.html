Trust And Service Boundaries

GEMS provides certain guidance and consulting services directly, while also serving as a trusted connection point to carefully selected providers within the GEMS Trusted Network. The purpose of the membership is not to suggest that GEMS itself performs every service a member may require, but rather that GEMS helps the member understand what they need, provides guidance where we can, and connects them with the right trusted resource when specialized assistance is required.

For services that require licensed, regulated, or otherwise specialized professional expertise, GEMS acts as the trusted guide and connector, while the actual professional service is provided by the appropriately qualified provider. The relevant GEMS service page and member workflow will always make this distinction clear, so members always understand who is providing the service, what GEMS' role is, and where professional responsibility begins and ends.

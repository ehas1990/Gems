COMPANION MEMBERSHIP

Companion — Need-To-Know Access

Companion members should not receive unrestricted access to the Trusted Network Directory. Instead, GEMS should act as the intermediary. When a Companion member identifies a need, GEMS determines which trusted partner or category is relevant and provides the appropriate information.

01

The member explains the need through the normal GEMS member workflow.

02

GEMS reviews the request and identifies an appropriate trusted partner or resource.

03

GEMS provides only the information necessary to address that need.

04

Where appropriate, GEMS makes the introduction or connection.

05

The member experience should feel helpful and personal—not like a restriction or denial of access.

NAVIGATOR MEMBERSHIP

Navigator — Full Trusted Network Access

Navigator members should receive access to the protected Trusted Network Directory as part of their membership.

Search or browse trusted partners by category.

View the partner information GEMS has approved for member presentation.

Identify the types of services or assistance offered.

View relevant location or service-area information where applicable.

Request an introduction or connection through GEMS when desired.

Receive deeper GEMS guidance when choosing among appropriate trusted resources.

The directory should not imply that every listed partner is appropriate for every member or situation. The directory is a trusted resource within the GEMS relationship; GEMS remains the guide.

PHILOSOPHY & INTEGRITY

How Our Network Works

Our network is built on trust, transparency, and human care — never commissions or commercial bidding.

Trusted Connections, Carefully Chosen

At GEMS, we believe that a trusted connection can make all the difference in a journey. Our Trusted Network brings together individuals, businesses, and professional service providers whom GEMS has carefully selected based on their experience, reputation, service quality, and alignment with the values we believe are important to our members.

GEMS does not simply provide a list of businesses. We build relationships with members of our network so that, when you need help beyond what GEMS provides directly, we can help you identify an appropriate resource and, when applicable, make the connection.

Our Role Is To Guide And Connect

GEMS may provide certain guidance and consulting services directly. For services outside the scope of GEMS, we may connect you with an appropriate member of our Trusted Network.

When you engage a Trusted Network provider, that provider is an independent business or professional and is not an employee, agent, or representative of GEMS unless expressly stated otherwise. The provider is responsible for the services they perform, the advice they provide, their professional decisions, and the terms of your engagement with them.

Where a service requires a licensed, certified, or otherwise qualified professional, the provider's qualifications and professional responsibilities remain with that provider. GEMS' role is to help you find and connect with an appropriate resource—not to replace the professional relationship between you and the provider.
